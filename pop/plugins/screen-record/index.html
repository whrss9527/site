Record Screen

Drag out an area, click a window or press Return to record the whole screen as MP4; can include the Mac’s sound or the microphone and show clicks and keystrokes, and the recording can then be turned into a GIF. Use it again while recording to stop.

How to use it

Select something, hold the right mouse button, swipe. The animation plays the steps one after another; click a step to see it again.

  1. No need to select anythingIt works wherever you are.
  2. Hold the right mouse buttonAbout a quarter of a second. A short click still opens the usual menu.
  3. Swipe to “Record Screen” and let goIt sits on the ring where you put it, and it’s always in All Actions.
  4. Drag out the area to recordOr click a window, or press Return for the whole screen.
  5. RecordingThe timer in the menu bar stops it.
  6. Saved as an MP4Show it in Finder, or turn it into a GIF.

What it does

Drag out an area, click a window, or press Return to record the whole screen. It’s saved as an MP4.

Record the Mac’s sound or the microphone, show clicks and the keys you press, and count down 3 seconds before it starts.

While recording, a timer sits in the menu bar; click it, or use Record Screen again, to stop.

When it’s done, show the file in Finder or convert it to a GIF right away.

Install it

Plugins aren’t part of the Pop download. Install this one when you need it.

1From Settings

In Pop’s Settings › Actions, find “Record Screen” under Plugins at the top and click Install. It downloads from Pop’s GitHub release in a few seconds and works right away, without a restart.

2Or from the ring

Open All Actions on the ring and search for “Record Screen”. Plugins that aren’t installed are listed after your actions: click it to install, and it runs right away if it can handle what you selected.

3Then use it your way

Put it on the ring in Settings › Ring, give it a shortcut, or run it from Shortcuts and scripts with its link:

pop://run?plugin=screenRecord

Uninstall it from the same list when you no longer need it: the plugin and its settings are deleted. After Pop updates, installed plugins are updated with it, and with iCloud sync on, what you install syncs to your other Macs.

More in Recording & Presenting

Voice Recorder

Record sound from the microphone and save it as an .m4a file in Downloads; while recording, a small bar at the top of the screen shows the time and level and can pause, and you can transcribe the recording afterwards. Use it again while recording to stop

Scrolling Screenshot

Select an area, then scroll down while Pop captures it into one long image: whole web pages, chat histories and long documents, with text recognition afterwards if you like. Use it again while capturing to finish

Show Keystrokes

Show the shortcuts you press (⌘C, ⇧⌘4, arrow keys and so on) at the bottom of the screen for demos and tutorials; ordinary typing isn’t shown. Use it again to turn it off. Screen recordings can include them too

Draw on Screen

Draw right on the screen for demos and tutorials: pen, highlighter, arrow, rectangle and ellipse. Strokes can fade after a few seconds or stay while you use the windows underneath, and screen recordings include them. Press Esc or use it again to finish

Camera Bubble

Show the camera in a small round window in a corner of the screen, so you appear in tutorials and demos. Drag to move it, scroll or double-click to resize, right-click to change the shape or camera. Use it again to close

Highlight Pointer

Put a yellow halo around the pointer that ripples when you click, so people can always find it in demos and tutorials. Use it again to turn it off
