Case

Convert between uppercase, lowercase, camelCase, snake_case and more.

Works with Selected textFree
See how it works Download Pop
UPPERCASE · lowercasecamelCase · PascalCasesnake_case · kebab-case

How to use it

Select something, hold the right mouse button, swipe. The animation plays the steps one after another; click a step to see it again.

  1. Select a name
  2. Hold the right mouse buttonAbout a quarter of a second. A short click still opens the usual menu.
  3. Swipe to “Case” and let goIt sits on the ring where you put it, and it’s always in All Actions.
  4. Every case at once
  5. Copy the snake_case oneOr put it back in place of the selection.

What it does

Select a word, a phrase or a variable name and see it in eight cases at once: UPPERCASE, lowercase, Capitalized, camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case and CONSTANT.

Names written in one piece are split into words first, acronyms included: getHTTPResponse becomes get, HTTP, Response.

Copy any line, or put it back in place of the selection.

Install it

Plugins aren’t part of the Pop download. Install this one when you need it.

1From Settings

In Pop’s Settings › Actions, find “Case” under Plugins at the top and click Install. It downloads from Pop’s GitHub release in a few seconds and works right away, without a restart.

2Or from the ring

Open All Actions on the ring and search for “Case”. Plugins that aren’t installed are listed after your actions: click it to install, and it runs right away if it can handle what you selected.

3Then use it your way

Put it on the ring in Settings › Ring, give it a shortcut, or run it from Shortcuts and scripts with its link:

pop://run?plugin=changeCase

Uninstall it from the same list when you no longer need it: the plugin and its settings are deleted. After Pop updates, installed plugins are updated with it, and with iCloud sync on, what you install syncs to your other Macs.
