Support

The fastest way to get help is an issue on GitHub — it’s public, so the answer helps the next person too. For anything private, write to me.

Pop

Right-click does nothing after an update? Settings › General › “Clear old authorization records”, then grant Accessibility again.

Meno

Not responding after an update? Settings › Permissions › “Reset and Grant Again”. For a report, use Settings › About › Copy Diagnostic Report.

Report a problem Known issues Release notes FAQ Privacy

Stox

Quotes not updating? Check the note at the bottom of the panel; Stox switches to its backup source automatically.

Proxi

Proxy not working? Click the speedometer in the panel to test every profile. Settings › Diagnose shows the proxy settings across the system and opens the folder with proxi.log.

Email

For questions you’d rather not post publicly, security reports, or anything about privacy and your data: whrss9527@gmail.com. I read everything; replies may take a few days. English or Chinese is fine.

A good report includes

  1. The app version (in its Settings or About page) and your macOS version.
  2. Apple silicon or Intel.
  3. What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  4. A screenshot or screen recording if it’s visual.

Before you write

  1. Update first. Each app can update itself; the fix may already be out.
  2. Permissions after an update. If an app that uses Accessibility (Pop, Meno) stops responding, remove it from System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility and add it again, or use the reset button in the app.
  3. “Can’t be opened” warnings. Current releases are notarized and open with a double-click. Very old versions were not: right-click the app and choose Open, or download the latest release.
